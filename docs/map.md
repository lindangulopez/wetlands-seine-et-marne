## How to use the map

(i) **Go to a wetland site.** Top left, the **Sites de classe A** menu lists the twelve largest confirmed wetlands, numbered as on [slide 6](wetlands.md). Pick one: the map zooms to it, switches on the wetland layer and opens the site's card (commune, area of confirmed wetland, protected site).

(ii) **Search.** Type at least two letters in the box under the menu. The list shows, first, matches among the class A sites, the 14 catchments, the Natura 2000 sites and the ZNIEFF; then communes and addresses from the national address base (*Base Adresse Nationale*). Click a result, or move to it with the Tab key and press Enter. Accents are optional: "episy" finds the Marais d'Episy.

(iii) **Change what you see.** The menu top right has two parts. The round buttons are the **backgrounds**: OpenStreetMap, Plan IGN, IGN aerial photos, or the simple world map from QGIS. The tick boxes are the **layers** you can lay on top: catchments, the département, dominant soils, land cover, wetlands, the numbered class A sites, Natura 2000, ZNIEFF and the GBIF species records.

(iv) **Read the colours.** When the wetlands, soils or land cover layer is on, its legend opens bottom right. Wetlands: purple for confirmed (class A), amber for probable (class B), blue for water (class D) and, outside Île-de-France, teal for the Seine-Normandie potential wetlands.

(v) **Click for details.** Click a catchment, a protected site, a numbered site or a species dot to open its card; Natura 2000 and ZNIEFF cards link to the INPN.

(vi) **On a phone.** Open the map full screen. Pinch to zoom; the layer menu folds into an icon top right; the information box is hidden to leave room for the map.

!!! note "Good to know"
    The wetland layer is a pre-rendered image at 40 m: it shows where to look, not legal limits. The class A and B classes cover Île-de-France only (DRIEAT, 2021); outside it, the teal areas come from a different, single-class source (AESN, 2016). The soil and land-cover layers are served live by the IGN Géoplateforme, so they need an internet connection and can take a few seconds.
