# Learn the species

The species on these maps come from a study aide-mémoire: the rules for delimiting a wetland in France, 24 indicator plants, six Natura 2000 habitat codes, 14 wintering waterbirds, nine invasive species, and the large-flowered sandwort of Fontainebleau.

They are also a set of flashcards with photos, in two steps: **Learn** (every card face up) then **Q&A** (photo or name, then *Show answer*).
