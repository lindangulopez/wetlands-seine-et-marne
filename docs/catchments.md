# 1 · The catchments that drain Seine-et-Marne

<p class="slide-lead">Fourteen river catchments, 9,318 km². Five of them (Almont, Voulzie, Yerres, Seine at Melun, Marne) lie mostly inside the département; the Loing, Ourcq and Petit Morin start well outside it.</p>

![Catchments draining Seine-et-Marne](maps/00-bassins.png)

(i) **How the area is drawn.** Every BD CARTO topographic catchment (zone hydrographique, Sandre code such as F437) that overlaps the département, 81 zones, grouped by the river they drain to. Their union covers all of Seine-et-Marne.

(ii) **Two large rivers frame it.** The Marne crosses the north from Château-Thierry to the Paris basin; the Seine enters by the Bassée, takes the Yonne at Montereau and the Loing at Saint-Mammès, then flows north past Melun.

(iii) **Why a catchment view.** Wetlands depend on what happens upstream: the Loing at Episy carries water from the Gâtinais in Loiret, and the Petit Morin from the Marais de Saint-Gond in the Marne département.
