# Wetlands of Seine-et-Marne, a catchment view

<p class="slide-lead">Rivers do not stop at the département boundary. These maps follow the 14 river catchments that drain Seine-et-Marne, 9,318 km² in all, 64 % of it inside the département, and look at their soils, vegetation and wetland species in open data.</p>

![Catchments draining Seine-et-Marne](maps/00-bassins.png)

## In five slides

(i) [Catchments](catchments.md): 81 BD CARTO hydrographic zones, grouped into 14 river catchments, from the Ourcq to the Loing.

(ii) [Soils](soils.md): Luvisols on the loess plateaus; hydromorphic soils, mostly Fluviosols, dominate 12.3 % of the area, along the valleys.

(iii) [Vegetation](vegetation.md): arable land covers 58.7 %, forest 21.8 %, and Natura 2000 sites 8.6 %.

(iv) [Species](species.md): where GBIF records the 24 wetland indicator plants, 14 wintering waterbirds and 9 invasive species of a study aide-mémoire.

(v) [Loing valley](loing.md): a close-up of the lower Loing and the Fontainebleau forest, with the large-flowered sandwort.

Then: the [interactive map](map.md), a [winter wetland story](story/index.md) told one post a day, [flashcards](flashcards.md) to learn the species, and the [sources and method](sources.md).
